A joint research group in China

We are a joint research group initiated by Peking University in 2025. We study how climate change and human activities reshape water quality and nutrient cycles across watersheds — from urban stormwater to global riverine fluxes to the ocean — by combining process-based watershed models, machine learning, and large-scale data synthesis. Our participating institutions include Peking University, Beijing Normal University, the Research Center for Eco-Environmental Sciences (CAS), Suzhou University of Science and Technology, and Hebei University of Engineering, whose members work together to bring field observation, modeling, and data science to shared questions.

  • Watershed hydrology & water quality
  • Global nutrient (N/P) cycles
  • Nonpoint source pollution
  • Machine learning + process models

About Me

{{ site.lab.pi }} {{ site.lab.pi_cn }}

I am Guo Liu, a postdoctoral researcher at Peking University. I lead the Watershed Environment Lab. My research sits at the interface of hydrology, biogeochemistry, and data science. I develop watershed models coupled with machine learning to understand and manage nonpoint source pollution, and I synthesize global datasets to quantify how humans have altered riverine nutrient fluxes to the ocean. My recent work on human alterations to global riverine phosphorus fluxes was published in Science Advances.
